About Me

A little about who I am

The story behind the code.

Hey there! I'm Ali, a final-year Software Engineering student at Sir Syed University in Karachi. Alongside my studies, I work part-time as an AI/ML intern at Lambda Theta, where I get to apply what I learn in class to real computer vision systems.

At Lambda Theta, I work on a computer vision system focused on activity detection in CCTV footage using tools like MMPose, keypoint detection, and YOLO. Beyond that, I help label datasets, train and evaluate models, and handle the annotation and data-processing work that keeps our ML pipelines running.

Outside of AI, I enjoy building full-stack web projects to sharpen my software engineering fundamentals — and when I'm away from the keyboard, you'll usually find me playing football.
